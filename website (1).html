A few things worth knowing.

Does Relay use AI to write my posts?

This version uses local sentence extraction and writing templates, not an AI model. It makes real, editable drafts directly in your browser without a subscription or API key. Use your editorial judgment to polish the result.

Can I paste a link or upload a PDF?

Paste the article text or relevant excerpts from your PDF into the source field. The studio doesn't fetch websites or parse files. You can add an original source URL separately for attribution.

Is my source text stored or published?

No. Draft processing happens locally and source text is not sent to a server or saved by this site. Refreshing clears the studio, so copy or download work you want to keep. Publishing is always up to you.
